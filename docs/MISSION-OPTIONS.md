# Our Mission Statement — voice options

Drafts for **Our Mission Statement.docx** (`mission` in `src/content/docs.ts`). Nothing in
`docs.ts` has been changed yet. Pick a direction (or mix parts) and the copywriter will set it in the file.

Each draft maps to the existing doc blocks: `title`, `subtitle`, `quote` (the mission sentence),
`h2` + `list` for beliefs, `h2` + `list` for process, and a closing `p`. Belief items are written as
"Claim. Why it matters." because the `list` block has no bold. Each reads in about 60–75 seconds.

Facts used: independent design & development studio; web design, development, branding; booking is
through the notebook on the desk; the form promises a reply within one business day. Process steps
and working promises (written scope, support after launch, etc.) are **not yet confirmed** by the
owner — see the checklist at the end.

---

## Option A — Confident & minimal

*Voice:* short, sure, no decoration; lets the work and the process do the talking.
*Suits:* owners who are busy, skim, and want proof you'll be easy to work with. Matches the exterior
headline most closely.

**Our Mission**
*d> Studio — Design & Development*

> We design and build websites that make small businesses easy to find, easy to trust and easy to choose.

**What we believe**
- Clear beats clever. Visitors decide in seconds. They should know what you do before they scroll.
- Fast is a feature. Every second a page takes to load is a chance for a customer to leave.
- Design and code belong together. The studio that designs your site builds it, so nothing gets lost in a handoff.
- Launch is the start. A website earns its keep over years, not on day one.

**How we work**
- Talk. A first call about your business, your customers and what the site needs to do.
- Plan. We agree on scope, timeline and cost before any work starts.
- Design. You see real pages early, while changes are still quick and cheap.
- Build and launch. We build it, test it on phones and desktops, and put it live.
- Stay on. We're here for fixes, updates and whatever you need next.

Ready when you are. Open the notebook on the desk to book a call. We reply within one business day.

## Option B — Warm & human

*Voice:* friendly, first-person-plural, reassuring; sounds like a person you'd enjoy working with.
*Suits:* first-time website buyers, local and family businesses, anyone nervous about the process.

**Our Mission**
*d> Studio — Design & Development*

> We build websites for small businesses the way we'd want our own built: with care, in plain language, and made to last.

**What we believe**
- You know your business best. Our job is to ask good questions and turn your answers into a site that sounds like you.
- Plain language, always. You'll understand every decision we make, and so will your customers.
- Small details build trust. A working phone link, clear opening hours, a page that loads fast. That's what makes a visitor feel at home.
- Good work takes good company. We'd rather do fewer projects well than rush many.

**How we work**
- We get to know you. A relaxed first call about what you do, who you serve and what's not working today.
- We agree the plan. Clear scope, timeline and cost, so there are no surprises.
- We design together. You'll see the site take shape and help steer it.
- We build and launch it. Tested, polished and live.
- We stick around. Your business will change, and your website should keep up.

If this sounds like the kind of studio you'd like to work with, the notebook on the desk is open. Leave us a note and we'll get back to you within one business day.

## Option C — Bold & editorial

*Voice:* punchy, opinionated, a little provocative; reads like a magazine manifesto.
*Suits:* businesses that want to stand out (hospitality, retail, creative brands) and value design taste.

**Our Mission**
*d> Studio — Design & Development*

> Most websites get scrolled past. We make the ones people stop for.

**What we believe**
- Ordinary is invisible. If your site looks like everyone else's, customers can't tell you apart.
- Every page has a job. Book, buy, call, visit. If a page doesn't move someone toward it, it goes.
- Taste is a business decision. Careful type, generous space and real restraint tell customers you take your work seriously.
- Design and build, one roof. What we draw is what ships. Nothing gets watered down between the two.

**How we work**
- Listen. We learn the business before we touch the brand.
- Decide. One clear direction, agreed in writing, before work begins.
- Make. Design and code move together, and you see progress early.
- Launch. Tested on every screen that matters, then live.
- Keep going. We measure what works and sharpen it.

Worth a conversation? Book a call from the notebook on the desk.

## Option D — Technical & precise

*Voice:* measured, specific, standards-led; builds trust through how things are done.
*Suits:* service firms, clinics, startups and owners who've been burned by a slow or broken site before.

**Our Mission**
*d> Studio — Design & Development*

> We design and build fast, accessible websites that turn visitors into customers and stay simple to run.

**What we believe**
- Speed is measured, not promised. We test load times on phones before launch, because that's where many of your customers arrive.
- Accessible by default. A site that works with a keyboard and a screen reader works better for everyone, and reaches more people.
- You should be able to run it. Updating hours, prices or photos shouldn't need a developer.
- Decisions belong in writing. Scope, timeline and cost are agreed before work starts.

**How we work**
- Discovery call. Your goals, customers, current site and what success looks like.
- Proposal. A written scope with timeline and cost.
- Design. Page layouts and a visual system, reviewed with you at each stage.
- Build and test. Clean code, checked for speed, accessibility and every common screen size.
- Launch and support. We put it live, hand over everything you need, and stay available after.

To start, book a call from the notebook on the desk. We reply within one business day.

## Recommendation

**Option A**, with Option C's mission line kept in reserve. A sits closest to the exterior headline
("We design and build websites worth visiting.") and speaks straight to a small-business owner deciding
whether to book. It's specific about outcomes (found, trusted, chosen) without hype. If the studio wants
more edge, swap A's quote for C's "Most websites get scrolled past. We make the ones people stop for."
The rest of A still works around it.

## To confirm before publishing (applies to all options)

- Scope, timeline and cost agreed in writing before work starts (A, B, C, D).
- Ongoing support after launch is offered (A, B, C, D).
- Same people design and build, with no outsourcing (A, C).

- "Fewer projects, done well" reflects how the studio actually takes on work (B).
